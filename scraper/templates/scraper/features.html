✉️ Outreach

Where the actual selling happens — email, social and WhatsApp, with every send recorded.

Compose & Send

Send a template to one lead or to a filtered batch. Merge fields fill in each business's own details, and sending is throttled so it does not look like a blast.

Every send automatically gets an open pixel, click-tracked links, an unsubscribe link, and a check against the suppression list. {{ stats.templates }} template{{ stats.templates|pluralize }} ready to use.
Open Compose →

Drip Sequences Automatic

Multi-step campaigns: first email today, follow-up in 3 days, another in a week. A background timer sends what is due every 10 minutes.

Stops by itself when the lead replies, changes status, or unsubscribes — so nobody gets a "just following up" after they already said yes. Most replies to cold email come from the second and third touch, not the first.
Open Sequences →

Social Outreach New

Work the {{ stats.with_social }} leads that have a Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn profile. Buttons open the profile and record the visit on the way.

Social has no open tracking, so messages and replies are logged with one click instead. A reply moves the lead to Interested automatically, and every touch shows on the lead's timeline.
Open Social Outreach →

WhatsApp Bulk

Builds pre-filled WhatsApp links for leads that have a phone number, so you can open a chat with the message already typed.

Best for Indian SMBs, who answer WhatsApp far more reliably than email. Log what happened from the Social Outreach screen.

Working quietly in the background

Things that run without anyone opening a screen.

Open & click tracking

An invisible pixel records opens; links are rewritten to record clicks before forwarding the reader on.

Caveat worth knowing: opens only register if the recipient loads images. Clicks are the more reliable signal.

Sequence sender

A timer checks every 10 minutes for sequence steps that have come due and sends them.

It takes a lock, so a slow batch cannot overlap the next run and send the same step twice.

Bounce handling

When a mail server rejects an address outright, that address is recorded as bounced and added to the suppression list.

Why it matters: repeatedly mailing dead addresses is one of the fastest ways to end up in spam folders for everyone else too.

Enrichment

Visits each lead's website and pulls out email addresses and social profiles, checking that the mail domain actually exists.

This is where the {{ stats.with_email }} email addresses and {{ stats.with_social }} social profiles came from.

Lead scoring

Scores each lead 0–100 on how complete its data is and how well the business rates.

Sort by score to work the most contactable leads first instead of going alphabetically.

Local to server sync

Pushes leads from your machine to this server over an authenticated API.

Only leads travel. On a lead that already exists here, sync fills empty fields and never overwrites a status, note or history added on the server.
